The Problem

Multi-brand creators don't have a tool problem.
They have a contamination problem.

Run more than one brand and every scheduler, inbox, and drafts folder becomes a place where the wrong voice leaks to the wrong audience.

🧺

Voices bleed in the wash

Your mystical brand's language shows up in your professional brand's post. One slip and an audience you spent years building starts to doubt what you are.

🎭

Wrong account, wrong post

Every multi-brand creator has posted to the wrong account at least once. Most tools make it easy — they pile all your identities into one dropdown.

🧠

Context-switching tax

Holding four brand voices in your head at once means none of them get your best work. Separation isn't a luxury — it's how each brand stays sharp.

Features

Brand Cubicles

Isolated workspaces per identity — voice profile, audience notes, lexicon, palette, and content history that never mix.

Bleed Check™

Real-time contamination scanning. Drafts are checked against every other brand's vocabulary before you publish.
