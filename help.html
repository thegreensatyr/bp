How Brandparent works

A cubicle is a room for one brand — its own voice, calendar, and lexicon. This walks through setting one up start to finish, in the order it actually makes sense to do it.

← Back to your cubicles
1. Create a cubicle 2. Getting to know you 3. Logo & theme 4. Draft & Bleed Check 5. Calendar 6. Connect accounts

1Create a cubicle

Click the + next to "Your Cubicles" in the sidebar, or + Create a cubicle if you're starting from empty. Give it a name and a color, save — everything below happens inside that room.

Start with the brand you know best. Your own is the fastest to fill in correctly, and it doubles as your working example when you're showing this to anyone else.

2Getting to know youLive

Open a cubicle's Voice Profile tab and click Start interview. Answer a few questions in plain language — no right format — then apply the results.

Not happy with an answer? Start over resets the chat without touching anything already saved.

3Logo & themeLive

Logo: upload one per cubicle (PNG, JPG, WEBP, or SVG, up to 5MB) — it replaces the initials badge everywhere that brand shows up. Theme: pick colors and fonts by hand, or click Detect theme from my site to pull them from a live website.

Auto-detect is best-effort — a heavily JavaScript-rendered site may not return much. Manual entry always works as a fallback.

4Draft a post & run Bleed CheckLive

Write in the Composer tab. Bleed Check runs as you type, comparing your draft against every other cubicle's vocabulary — not just this one's never-says.

✨ Optimize caption rewrites your draft in this cubicle's voice. Schedule adds it to that brand's calendar.

5CalendarLive

Each cubicle keeps its own calendar — nothing here is shared across brands.

6Connect accountsPartial

Connections are per-cubicle — connecting an account for one brand never gives another brand access to it.

  • Facebook & Instagram — live. Instagram posts need an image URL; Facebook doesn't.
  • Bluesky — live. Generate a free app password at bsky.app and connect — no developer approval needed.
  • TikTok — connects, but posts stay private (visible only to you) until the app passes TikTok's review.
  • LinkedIn — not connectable yet; the developer app hasn't been created.

If a platform isn't ready yet, draft and schedule anyway — the post is ready to fire the moment that connection goes live.
