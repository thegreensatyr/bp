Privacy Policy

Last updated July 31, 2026
Plain-language summary written by the Brandparent team, not a law firm. If you're relying on this for a compliance decision (GDPR, CCPA, or otherwise), have your own counsel review it.

1. What we collect

DataWhy
Email address, password (hashed)To create and secure your account.
Cubicle details — brand name, tone, audience, signature phrases, lexiconTo power the voice profile and Bleed Check features you set up.
Drafts and post content you writeTo save your work, run Bleed Check, and publish or schedule posts.
Access tokens for social accounts you connect (Facebook, Instagram, Bluesky, LinkedIn, TikTok, etc.)To post to that platform on your behalf, for the one cubicle you connected it to.
Basic usage data (login times, error logs)To keep the service running and debug problems.

2. What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data.
  • We don't share one cubicle's connected-account credentials or content with another cubicle, even within your own account — that separation is the whole point of the product.
  • We don't post anything to a connected account unless you initiate it (via "Publish now") or schedule it yourself.

3. Where it's stored

Brandparent runs on Supabase (Postgres + authentication), with access to your data restricted by row-level security so only your own account can read or write it. Automated scheduled publishing runs inside that same database on a fixed schedule, using a secret credential that isn't exposed publicly.

4. Third parties we send data to

When you connect a social account and publish or schedule content, we send that content (and, if included, an image URL) directly to the platform you connected — for example, Meta's Graph API for Facebook/Instagram, Bluesky's AT Protocol servers, LinkedIn's API, or TikTok's Content Posting API. We use Google's Gemini API (or Anthropic's Claude API, once configured) to power the optional "Optimize caption" feature; the draft text you submit for optimization is sent to that provider to generate a rewritten version, per their own data-use terms.

5. Your choices

  • Disconnect any social account any time from the Connections tab, or by revoking access from that platform's own account settings.
  • Delete a cubicle to remove its drafts, voice profile, and connected-account records.
  • Request full account deletion by emailing us — we'll remove your account data within a reasonable time.

6. Cookies

We use only what's needed to keep you logged in (an authentication session). We don't run third-party ad-tracking cookies.

7. Children

Brandparent isn't intended for anyone under 18. We don't knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes

We'll update the date at the top of this page whenever this policy changes.
