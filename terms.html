1. What BrandParent is

BrandParent.app ("BrandParent," "we," "us") is a tool that helps people who run more than one brand or project keep each one's voice, visuals, and social media accounts separate. You create a "cubicle" for each brand, and BrandParent helps you draft, check, schedule, and publish content for it — including, if you choose to connect them, posting directly to social platforms on your behalf.
