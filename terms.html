1. What Brandparent is

Brandparent.app ("Brandparent," "we," "us") is a tool that helps people who run more than one brand, project, or public identity keep each one's voice, visuals, and social media accounts separate. You create a "cubicle" for each brand, and Brandparent helps you draft, check, schedule, and publish content for it — including, if you choose to connect them, posting directly to social platforms on your behalf.
